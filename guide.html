Good to know

Our village

Litsarda

A small, peaceful stone village of about 100 people, 280 m up on a green ridge in Apokoronas, 25 km east of Chania. On the square you'll find the church of the Virgin Mary (Panagia), the village kafenion and the well-known pizzeria, which also has a small shop for basics. Bigger supermarkets, a bakery, pharmacy and ATM are 5 minutes away in Vamos.

Ilingas (Iligas)

A small pebble cove near Hora Sfakion on the Libyan Sea, with crystal-clear turquoise water that's warmer than the north coast — great for swimming and snorkelling. Sheltered when the north wind blows. Paid sunbeds, a beach bar and kayak rentals; parking is limited, so arrive before noon.

South coast · about 1 hr 15 min

Preveli

One of Crete's most magical beaches: a river winds through a palm forest into the Libyan Sea, so you can swim in cool fresh water and warm sea on the same day, and wade upstream between the palms. It's a wild beach with a small café — bring water and snacks. Park at Dionyssos Beach, 10–15 minute walk; the historic Preveli Monastery is just up the road.

West coast · about 1 hr 45 min

Falasarna

A long sweep of golden sand and crystal-clear turquoise water on Crete's far west coast, regularly voted one of the best beaches in Europe. Sunbeds and beach bars at the main stretch, quieter coves at either end, and the most spectacular sunsets on the island — stay for the evening. Ruins of the ancient harbour town of Phalasarna are at the northern end. Pairs well with Gramboussa taverna nearby.

Full-day hike · about 1 hr 45 min drive

Samaria Gorge

One of Europe's great walks: about 16 km (5–7 hours, mostly downhill) through a national park, from Xyloskalo on the Omalos plateau past towering cliffs and the famous "Iron Gates" down to Agia Roumeli on the Libyan Sea. From there, take the ferry to Chora Sfakion and a bus or taxi back. Start early, bring water, sturdy shoes and cash for the entrance fee. Organised day tours with transport are an easy option.

Day trip · south coast · about 2 hr 30 min drive

Matala

A laid-back beach village on the Libyan Sea, famous for the ancient caves carved into the cliffs above the bay — Roman tombs that became a 1960s hippie hideaway. Climb up for the views (good shoes needed; small entrance fee), swim in the bay, and stay for sunset at a waterfront taverna. A long drive, so best as an overnight or combined with Phaistos palace nearby.

Map
South coast hike · about 1 hr drive

Chora Sfakion → Sweetwater Beach → Loutro

A stunning stretch of the E4 coastal trail. From the road above Chora Sfakion it's about 30 minutes along the cliffs to Sweetwater (Glyka Nera) Beach — clear turquoise water fed by freshwater springs — then roughly another hour to Loutro, a car-free whitewashed village. Take the ferry back to Chora Sfakion. Rocky path: wear proper shoes and bring water and sun protection.

Chora SfakionSweetwaterLoutro
South coast hike · about 1 hr 15 min drive

Aradena Gorge

One of Crete's most dramatic — and much quieter than Samaria. Start at the old stone bridge in Aradena village and walk down through the gorge to Marmara Beach (allow 3–4 hours), then walk or take the boat to Loutro. Boulders and a few chain-assisted sections: good hiking shoes, water and an early start are a must.
